# CP06 numerical replay boundary

The fixed archived OMEGA record names pca/11. A clean CPython 3.13.5 / Linux environment with NumPy 2.3.5 and SciPy 1.17.0 completed the unchanged numerical synthetic kernels through two confirmation banks. Literal validation selection was pca/20, so the strict reference comparison failed although every numeric result leaf met atol=1e-10 and rtol=1e-8. A second clean invocation in the same installed environment produced an identical result-file hash.

The historical minimum validation loss for pca/11 was 0.6422895236152675. The CP06 minimum for pca/20 was 0.642289523613331. Many PCA widths lie on a near-tied numerical plateau. These observations are consistent with numerical sensitivity of the selection rule, but no controlled factorial experiment isolated the exact effect of Python, BLAS, CPU, floating-point ordering or runtime version. The R3 archive's environment record does not by itself identify the environment that originally measured R2 synthetic outputs, since R3 retained those measurements rather than rerunning them.

Do not force the refit selection back to 11 or silently change the historical record to 20. A historical locked-model scoring mode, a prospective tie-handling rule and a new fit are different procedures. None is substituted for the present replay. The two repeated CP06 results are not two independent confirmation studies.

The public manuscript must disclose this selection mismatch. Its historical scores and pca/11 label remain attributed to the original measurement, while the CP06 replay is separately dated. 'Code executes' and 'all original categorical outputs reproduce' are distinct release gates; the second remains open.
